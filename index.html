What connects the work

My research combines protein engineering, dynamic molecular measurements, and human-relevant in vitro models. I am interested in functions that become apparent only when we observe biology across space and time.

Through Nuuk Bio, I aim to bring optical phenotyping into preclinical discovery workflows and build physiological datasets for data-driven models of biology.
