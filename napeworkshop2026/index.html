Assumptions & source

The solid curve solves 1:1 equilibrium binding with both partners conserved: bound = 2 S L / [S + L + Kd + √((S + L + Kd)² − 4 S L)]. Concentrations are total concentrations. All sensors are binding-competent. Intensity assumes fixed OFF/ON brightnesses of 1/5 arbitrary units per molecule and constant detection.

Jarmoskaite et al. emphasize controlling incubation time and limiting-component concentration. This sandbox assumes equilibrium has already been reached; it does not test that assumption. In the stoichiometric titration regime, a curve can poorly constrain Kd even with the correct equation. How to measure and evaluate binding affinities.
